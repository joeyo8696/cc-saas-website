Most intake platforms stop at lead capture. A chatbot qualifies the prospect, a webform collects the details, Waypoint scores the case — and then what? The lead hits "request an agent" and enters a black hole. No queue. No visibility. No accountability. The firm that calls back in two hours loses to the one that called back in two minutes.

Today we're shipping the answer: **the Case Compass Agent Workforce Platform**.

This is a complete suite of tools for your intake team — everything they need from the moment a lead requests a live agent through the moment a retainer is signed. It ships inside the same Case Compass dashboard your team already uses. No new apps. No new logins.

Here's what's in it.

---

## Live Transfer Inbox

When a lead qualifies above your threshold and requests an agent — by phone, chatbot, or web form — they appear instantly in the Live Transfer Inbox.

Every row shows the lead's name, intake form, wait time, current status, and assigned agent. Agents click to claim and answer a transfer without ever leaving the dashboard.

![Case Compass Live Transfer Inbox showing real-time queue with transfer status, wait times, and agent assignments](/images/agent-platform-live-transfer-inbox.png)

The queue updates in real time. A new transfer appears the instant a lead hits the request button — no refresh needed. Live counters at the top of the queue show how many leads are waiting, in progress, and completed today across your entire team.

When an agent answers, the lead profile opens with the full conversation history on screen — every chatbot answer, every intake field, every contact detail — while the live call continues. No scrambling for context. No putting a client on hold to look something up.

## Browser Softphone with RingCentral

Your intake team can now make and receive calls directly inside Case Compass — powered by your existing RingCentral account.

![Case Compass RingCentral integration settings showing configured credentials, dial mode selection, inbound number, and call recording toggle](/images/agent-platform-ringcentral-settings.png)

Connect in minutes. Choose your dial mode:

- **Browser Softphone** — agents talk directly in the browser. A floating panel stays visible across every page in the dashboard. Mute, hold, and transfer are built in.
- **RingOut** — bridges the call to an agent's desk phone or mobile. Works with any existing handset.

Every call — regardless of dial mode — is automatically logged to the lead, recorded, and attributed to the correct marketing source. No manual entry. No missing records.

When an inbound call comes in from a number that matches an existing lead, Case Compass routes the agent directly to that lead's profile. If the caller is new, they land on the call intake screen with all the context they need to start qualifying.

## Agent SOPs & Checklists

Intake quality is only as consistent as the team having the right script at the right moment. Agent SOPs solve that.

![Case Compass SOP editor showing a Live Transfer Empathy and Compliance Script with document type, markdown content, and form assignment tags](/images/agent-platform-sop-editor.png)

Create two types of SOPs:

- **Documents** — rich text scripts with opening lines, objection handling, required disclosures, and compliance language. Formatted for fast scanning mid-call.
- **Checklists** — step-by-step tasks with required fields and completion tracking.

SOPs are assigned at the form level. A personal injury agent gets the PI script. A mass tort agent gets the MDL checklist. The right guidance surfaces automatically — no searching, no switching tabs.

When you update a script, it publishes to the entire team immediately. No email chains. No retraining sessions. Every agent is on the same page the moment you hit save.

## Speed-to-Lead Dashboard

You can't improve what you can't see. The speed-to-lead dashboard gives supervisors a live, filterable view of how fast their team is responding to leads.

![Case Compass Speed-to-Lead Dashboard showing average response time, SLA compliance percentage, response time histogram, daily trend chart, and agent leaderboard](/images/agent-platform-speed-dashboard.png)

Six metrics, all live:

- **Average response time** — how long leads are waiting before first agent contact, across today, this week, this month, or a custom range
- **SLA compliance %** — set your target response window (e.g. under 5 minutes) and see live what percentage of leads are being reached within it
- **Response distribution** — a histogram showing exactly where delays cluster, broken into buckets from under 1 minute to 15+ minutes
- **Daily trend chart** — rolling daily average response time, color-coded by SLA tier
- **Agent leaderboard** — per-agent breakdown of calls, average response time, SLA compliance, conversions, and conversion rate
- **Queue depth & missed** — live counters for how deep the queue is and how many leads were missed today

When response time spikes, supervisors see it immediately — not in an end-of-day report.

## Outbound Callback Queue

Not every lead can be reached on the first try. The Outbound Callback Queue makes sure no promised callback is forgotten.

![Case Compass Outbound Callback Queue showing pending callbacks with wait times and a Schedule Outbound Callback modal with lead selector, date, time, and agent assignment](/images/agent-platform-callback-queue.png)

Schedule a callback for any lead, assign it to a specific agent, set the time, and add context notes. The queue tracks three states: **Pending**, **Attempted**, and **Completed** — with elapsed wait time on every row so supervisors know which callbacks are aging.

Agents click "Call now" to launch the softphone directly from the queue. No number copying. No context switching. One click from the queue to a live call with full lead context on screen.

## Lead Profile: The Full Picture on Every Call

When a live transfer is answered or an inbound call connects, the agent gets everything they need on one screen.

![Case Compass lead profile showing live transfer panel with agent chat, intake answers from the chatbot, phone call history with recordings, and SOPs embedded inline](/images/agent-platform-lead-profile-lt.png)

The lead profile during a call shows:

- The live transfer chat or active call on the left
- The full intake conversation history on the right rail — every question the bot asked, every answer the lead gave
- Phone call history with recordings and post-call summaries
- Agent SOPs and checklists embedded inline below the call log
- A "Take over chat" button to step directly into a bot conversation as a human agent
- One-click resend of the chatbot link via email or SMS

Agents walk into every call prepared. No scrambling. No "let me pull up your file."

## Getting Started

The Agent Workforce Platform is available now. To get set up:

1. **Live Transfer Inbox** — enabled automatically if your account has live transfer configured
2. **RingCentral** — go to **Settings → Integrations → RingCentral** and connect your credentials. Choose your dial mode and configure your inbound number.
3. **SOPs & Checklists** — go to **Agent SOPs** in your dashboard. Create your first document or checklist and assign it to a form.
4. **Speed-to-Lead Dashboard** — available under **Reports → Speed to Lead**
5. **Callback Queue** — available on any lead profile under the **Schedule Callback** action

If you'd like a walkthrough specific to your team's workflow, [schedule time with us](/contact) and we'll configure everything together.
