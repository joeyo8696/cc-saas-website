Hi there,

Most intake platforms stop at lead capture. A chatbot qualifies the prospect, a webform collects the details — and then the lead hits "request an agent" and enters a black hole. No queue. No visibility. No accountability.

The firm that calls back in two hours loses to the one that called back in two minutes. Today, we're shipping the answer.

New Feature

Live Transfer Inbox

When a lead qualifies and requests an agent — by phone, chatbot, or web form — they appear instantly in the queue. No delay. No manual routing. Every row shows the lead's name, intake form, wait time, status, and assigned agent. Agents click once to claim and answer.

⚡ Instant, live updates

New transfers appear the moment a lead hits the request button. No polling. No page refresh needed.

📅 Full context on pickup

Intake form, wait time, and conversation history are pre-loaded. Agents walk into every call prepared.
New Integration

Browser Softphone — Powered by RingCentral

Your intake team can now make and receive calls directly inside Case Compass — no app required. Connect your existing RingCentral account in minutes. Choose Browser Softphone to talk in any browser, or RingOut to bridge to a desk or mobile phone. Every call is automatically logged, recorded, and attributed.

Zero extra cost for existing RingCentral customers

If your firm already uses RingCentral, connecting to Case Compass uses your existing account and phone numbers — no new licensing, no number porting.
New Feature

Agent SOPs & Checklists

Intake quality is only as consistent as your least experienced agent having the right script at the right moment. SOPs put your firm's playbook directly in the agent's workspace — automatically, for every call. Update a script and it publishes to the entire team immediately.

📄 Document SOPs

Rich-text scripts with opening lines, objection handling, and compliance disclosures. Formatted for fast scanning mid-call.

✅ Checklist SOPs

Step-by-step tasks with completion tracking. Supervisors see completion percentage across the team in real time.
New Dashboard

Speed-to-Lead Dashboard

The first firm to respond wins. The speed-to-lead dashboard gives supervisors a live, filterable view of exactly how fast their team is responding — and where delays are coming from. Set an SLA target and watch compliance in real time.

What you can track

Avg. response time — today, week, month, or custom range

SLA compliance % — live percentage reached within your target window

Agent leaderboard — per-agent calls, avg. response, SLA %, and conversions
New Feature

Outbound Callback Queue

Not every lead can be reached on the first attempt. The Callback Queue makes sure no promised callback is forgotten. Schedule for any lead, assign to an agent, set the time, add notes. Agents click "Call now" to launch the softphone directly from the queue — one click to a live call with full context on screen.

Works With Your Stack

Every call logged, every SOP completed, every intake answered — pushed automatically to your CMS. Your team works in Case Compass; your case file stays up to date without anyone copying anything.

Getting Started

1

Live Transfer Inbox

Enabled automatically if live transfer is configured. Visit Live Transfers in your dashboard.
2

RingCentral Softphone

Go to Settings → Integrations → RingCentral. Enter your credentials, choose your dial mode, and configure your inbound number.
3

Agent SOPs & Checklists

Go to Agent SOPs in your dashboard. Click + New SOP, choose Document or Checklist, write your content, and assign to a form.
4

Speed Dashboard & Callback Queue

Reports → Speed to Lead for the dashboard. Schedule callbacks from any lead profile via Schedule Callback.
