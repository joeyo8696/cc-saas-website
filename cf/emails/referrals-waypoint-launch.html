New Feature

Waypoint — AI-Powered Lead Scoring

Not every lead deserves equal attention. Without a systematic way to evaluate them, the loudest or most recent cases get follow-up — not the strongest ones. Waypoint fixes that.

How It Works

The moment an intake is submitted, Waypoint automatically evaluates it against your firm's criteria — case value, liability, medical treatment, urgency, red flags, and more — and surfaces a score in your dashboard.

Built around your standards — not a generic template. Your criteria, your scoring logic.

Consistent evaluation at scale — every intake gets the same rigorous assessment, every time.

Score on intake or on demand — runs automatically or triggered manually from the lead record.

Privacy by Architecture — BYOK

Waypoint uses your firm's own AI API credentials. Your intake data flows directly between your account and the AI provider — never through a shared Case Compass pool, never used to train models. Your data stays yours.

Why These Features Belong Together

When a referral partner submits a lead through their portal, Waypoint can score it automatically before your team even opens the record. Your intake staff sees a case score alongside the lead's information — so follow-up is prioritized, not arbitrary.

Every referred lead is tracked. Every intake is evaluated. No cases fall through the cracks.
