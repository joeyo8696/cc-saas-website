Hi there,

This past month was one of our biggest release cycles yet. We shipped improvements across nearly every part of the platform — from eSign and intake forms to embedding, integrations, and partner onboarding. Here's a quick look at what's new.

eSign

eSign got a significant upgrade

Several improvements make the signing experience smoother for your clients and simpler to manage on your end.

Multiple documents in one step — send a full retainer package for signature in a single eSign block, instead of splitting it across multiple steps.

Clients can download their signed documents — completed docs are available for download directly from the client portal.

SMS reminders for pending signatures — nudge claimants who haven't signed yet via text, not just email.

Signed documents auto-attached to notifications — when a signature completion email goes out, the PDF is already attached.
Intake & Forms

More control over your intake experience

Six new improvements give you more flexibility over how forms look, how leads are captured, and how data flows.

Export Leads

Download your leads directly from the dashboard — for reporting, co-counsel sharing, or analysis.

Skip the Welcome Screen

Drop claimants straight into the first question — reducing drop-off for high-intent traffic.

Drag to Reorder Options

Reorder answer choices in your forms by dragging — no more deleting and recreating.

Media Library

Upload and reuse images across your forms and communication templates from a central library.

Auto Campaign Tracking

New traffic sources and campaigns are created automatically — no manual setup when launching new ads.

Mobile Detection

Captures whether each lead came in on mobile or desktop — better context without any extra work.
Webform & WordPress

Embed anywhere

A new embed SDK lets you drop a Case Compass intake form inline on any website — no redirects, no popups. Plus, an official WordPress plugin makes setup a single click.
Waypoint AI

Smarter scoring

Age-based intake rules, bulk criteria assignment across all forms, and a new status message that keeps claimants informed while scoring runs — all live now.

Also live

Referral partner self-onboarding — new partners receive a secure invite link and can activate their own portal without any setup from your team.

Litify integration improvements — practice areas now sync as case types, campaigns sync automatically, originating URL is passed with every lead, and webform submissions are correctly labeled in Litify.

And a number of fixes & polish items

Back button behavior, eSign document state, embedded webform display, timeline entry titles, lead profile reliability, address autocomplete, and template name display — all resolved.
