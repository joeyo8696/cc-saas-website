Common Questions

We've Heard It Before.
Here Are the Answers.

Do I have to pay anything upfront?
No. Harbor Law Group works on a contingency fee basis — we only get paid if we win your case. There are no upfront costs, no retainer fees, and no out-of-pocket expenses.
What if my employer says the injury was my fault?
California workers' comp is a no-fault system. Even if you contributed to the accident, you are generally still entitled to benefits. Don't let your employer or their insurer discourage you from filing.
How long do I have to file a claim?
In California, you generally have one year from the date of injury to file a workers' comp claim. However, you should report the injury to your employer within 30 days to avoid complications. Contact us as soon as possible.
What benefits can I receive?
You may be entitled to: medical treatment coverage, temporary disability payments (while you can't work), permanent disability benefits, supplemental job displacement benefits, and death benefits for dependents in fatal cases.
My employer doesn't have workers' comp insurance. What now?
You still have options. California's Uninsured Employers Benefits Trust Fund (UEBTF) may cover your claim. You may also have grounds for a civil lawsuit against your employer. Contact us — we'll figure out the best path forward.
