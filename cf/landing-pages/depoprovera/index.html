Common Questions

What firms ask before getting started

What documents do you collect to confirm Depo-Provera use?
We collect and verify: injection records from clinics or pharmacies, insurance claims and EOBs showing DMPA administration, provider notes referencing Depo-Provera, and pharmacy dispensing records. We normalize terminology across providers and build a complete exposure timeline with start date, stop date, duration, and last injection date.
Can you handle claimants who don't have records yet?
Yes. Case Compass captures provider names, clinic locations, and approximate dates of service to facilitate record requests on the claimant's behalf. We track request status, follow up automatically, and score cases on completeness — so you always know which leads are viable versus pending documentation.
How do you score "likely viable" vs "needs more proof"?
Our scoring engine evaluates: confirmed Depo-Provera use (duration, recency, last injection date), confirmed meningioma diagnosis (MRI/CT, surgical or pathology report), exposure-to-diagnosis latency, treatment intensity (watchful waiting vs surgery vs radiation), and symptom severity. Each dimension is scored and weighted to produce an overall litigability score with a clear evidence gap report.
Do you integrate with Litify, Clio, Salesforce, or other platforms?
Yes. Case Compass is CRM and case management agnostic. We push structured case data into Litify, Salesforce, Filevine, SmartAdvocate, Clio, MyCase, CASEpeer, TrialWorks, and other platforms via API. You receive fully structured, exportable case packets (CSV and PDF) with complete audit trails and source attribution.
Can the call center do live transfers for high-score claims?
Yes. Case Compass provides call center intake automation that your call center team can use to perform warm live transfers to your attorney team for leads that meet your viability threshold in real time. Lower-scoring leads can be routed to automated callback sequences, follow-up flows, or a pending records queue — keeping your attorneys focused on the highest-value claims.
