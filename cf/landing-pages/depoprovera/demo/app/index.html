Common Questions

Here Are the Answers.

What is the link between Depo-Provera and meningioma?
A 2024 study published in the British Medical Journal found that women who used injectable medroxyprogesterone acetate (Depo-Provera) for more than one year had a significantly higher risk of developing intracranial meningioma. The FDA subsequently updated Depo-Provera's labeling to include this risk. Lawsuits are now pending in MDL 3140.
Do I have to pay anything upfront?
No. Murphy Law handles Depo-Provera cases on a contingency fee basis. You pay nothing unless we win your case. There are no upfront costs, no retainer fees, and no out-of-pocket expenses — ever.
How long do I have to file a claim?
Statutes of limitations vary by state — typically 2 to 3 years from the date of diagnosis or when you reasonably should have known about the connection. Contact us as soon as possible. Even if you're unsure of your timeline, we can help you assess your options.
I only used Depo-Provera for a short time. Can I still file?
The strongest cases involve use of one year or more, but cases with shorter use periods may still qualify depending on the circumstances of your diagnosis and treatment. Complete our free case review and we'll give you an honest assessment.
What compensation could I receive?
Potential compensation may include medical expenses (past and future), lost wages and loss of earning capacity, pain and suffering, and costs related to ongoing care or rehabilitation. Each case is unique — our attorneys will assess the specific value of your claim.
