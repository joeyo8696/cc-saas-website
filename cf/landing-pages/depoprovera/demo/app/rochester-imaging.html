Common Questions

Imaging Intake FAQ

Do I need a referral to schedule?
Most advanced studies (MRI, CT) require an order from your referring provider. Our intake captures that information so front desk can verify before your appointment.
What if I have an implant or pacemaker?
Tell us during intake. MRI safety screening is part of the workflow — our clinical team reviews implant details before you are scheduled for MRI.
How long does the intake take?
Most patients finish in about five minutes. After intake you can pick an available appointment slot online.
Will my doctor get the results?
Yes. Reports are sent to your referring provider. Typical turnaround for routine studies is within 48 hours; urgent studies are prioritized.
